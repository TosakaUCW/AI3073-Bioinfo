Key genes in the network map to well-defined functions on GeneCards:
\textbf{CALM2} encodes calmodulin, a Ca$^{2+}$ sensor regulating many enzymes and ion channels;
\textbf{SNAP25} mediates synaptic vesicle exocytosis;
\textbf{PAFAH1B1 (LIS1)} controls neuronal migration via dynein--microtubule regulation;
\textbf{FTH1} is the ferritin heavy chain for cellular iron storage;
\textbf{SLC24A1 (NCKX1)} is a K$^{+}$-dependent Na$^{+}$/Ca$^{2+}$ exchanger essential for phototransduction;
\textbf{GNAS} encodes a G-protein $\alpha$-subunit driving cAMP signaling;
and multiple mitochondrial energy genes---\textbf{COX5A}/\textbf{COX7A2} (complex IV),
\textbf{SLC25A4}/\textbf{ANT1} (ADP/ATP carrier), \textbf{MDH1} (malate dehydrogenase)---underline enriched oxidative-phosphorylation and metabolic control in the hub set.
