    \item \textbf{Fish Trichohyalin-like Proteins:} The sequences \texttt{XP\_069502281.1} and \texttt{XP\_069493420.1} are trichohyalin-like proteins from the fish \textit{Astyanax mexicanum}.

    \item \textbf{Human MLH1:} The human MLH1 protein (\texttt{sp|P40692|MLH1\_HUMAN}) stands alone as a functionally distinct DNA mismatch repair protein.
